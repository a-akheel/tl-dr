A logistics customer wants to give warehouse supervisors a consistent view of shipment exceptions. Supervisors currently switch between email alerts and a carrier dashboard to identify delayed deliveries. The customer needs a dashboard that combines carrier events with internal shipment identifiers. Carrier events are available through a webhook integration. The initial deployment will cover one warehouse and two carrier partners. The integration must handle duplicate events without creating duplicate exceptions. The customer also requires access controls so each supervisor sees only assigned warehouses. A proof of concept will demonstrate event ingestion, exception filtering, and role-based access. The operations lead will evaluate whether the dashboard makes exception triage easier during a supervised trial. The next workshop will confirm webhook authentication and shipment identifier mapping with the carrier teams.
