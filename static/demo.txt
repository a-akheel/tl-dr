The customer is consolidating support requests from three regional teams into a single service platform. Each team currently maintains separate spreadsheets, which causes duplicate tickets and unclear ownership. The proposed solution connects the existing customer relationship system to the service platform through a REST API. A pilot will include the European support team and run for four weeks. The pilot must preserve customer identifiers and assign every imported ticket to a named owner. Support managers will review a daily report of failed imports and retry eligible records. Single sign-on is required before the pilot can expand to the other regions. The customer will compare duplicate ticket rates and average resolution times before and after the pilot. These measurements will determine whether the integration is ready for broader deployment. The next step is a discovery workshop with the support lead and the identity administrator.
